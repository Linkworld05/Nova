# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive and welcoming. Critique code and behavior rather
than attacking people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and sharing
someone else's private information are not acceptable.

Keep reports reproducible and disagreements technical. Maintainers may remove
harmful content, close disruptive discussions or restrict participation. Use
GitHub's reporting tools for platform abuse. For project-specific concerns,
contact the repository's listed maintainers through an available private channel;
do not publish personal details to request moderation.
